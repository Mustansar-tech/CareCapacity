description: User's boundary for production design-system work and preservation of SUR BI.

Standardise Care Capacity while preserving its existing workflows and product identity. Leave SUR Group BI unchanged for now; the user wants its design handled separately.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a scalable shared design system for an already-live app, not a rebuild, and excluded SUR BI.

**How to apply:** Scope proposed themes/components to Care Capacity, including overlays. Treat shared global styles as a cross-product regression risk. Verify BI remains unchanged rather than redesigning it as part of Care Capacity work.

For design audits, inspect authenticated screens and interactions when the user authorises access; do not present a login-page or source-only review as a complete application audit.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked for their signed-in app to be audited properly after the earlier source-based review.

**How to apply:** State the actual role, screen and interaction coverage. Separate browser-confirmed defects from source observations and untested states. Keep credentials and identifiable staff/client screenshots out of audit deliverables.

Subject: Platform Update — Dashboard & Capacity Outlook improvements now live

Hi team,

We've shipped a focused set of improvements to two key pages on the Care Capacity Dashboard. Everything is live now — just refresh your browser to see the changes.

Here's a summary of what's new.

──────────────────────────────────────────
HOME DASHBOARD
──────────────────────────────────────────

▸ Redesigned header
  The opening section now shows a clean "Welcome back — [Branch Name]" headline
  with a "This week at a glance" badge and a short description beneath it.

▸ Controls moved to the bottom
  Upload New Data, Process Data, week navigation, and the last-sync timestamp
  are now in a single row pinned to the bottom of the panel — out of the way
  until you need them.

▸ Chart labels now show dates, not week numbers
  The capacity vs demand chart now displays short dates like "27 Jul" instead
  of "Wk 31", so you no longer need to mentally translate week numbers.

▸ Removed clutter
  The short-days pill row, stat chips (supply %, demand met, capacity lost),
  and the old status/greeting narrative have all been removed.


──────────────────────────────────────────
CAPACITY OUTLOOK
──────────────────────────────────────────

▸ Stage badges always visible
  PVG, REF1, and REF2 badges now appear on every joiner row at all times —
  red when not yet completed, green once saved. No more expanding rows just
  to check what's outstanding.

▸ KPI cards reordered
  The four summary cards are now in workflow order:
  In Pipeline → Available Changes → Staff Leaving → Running Net

▸ Risk card removed
  The standalone Risk card has been removed from the KPI row to reduce noise.

▸ Confidence % removed from joiner modal
  The Confidence % field has been removed from the joiner edit modal to
  simplify the data entry workflow.


──────────────────────────────────────────

All changes are live now. Just refresh the dashboard to see them.

✦  Platform Update · 27 July 2026

What's new in your dashboard

We've updated two key pages — the Home dashboard and Capacity Outlook — to make your workforce data cleaner and easier to act on.

Hi team,

We've shipped a focused round of improvements to the Home dashboard and Capacity Outlook pages. The changes are live now — here's a quick summary of everything that's changed and why.

Page 1
Home Dashboard
Redesigned hero header
The opening section now shows a clear "Welcome back — Branch Name" headline with your active branch name highlighted in green. A "This week at a glance" pill badge and a short description sit beneath it. Cleaner, faster to read at a glance.
Controls moved to the bottom
Upload New Data, Process Data, week navigation and the sync timestamp are now all in a single row pinned to the bottom of the panel — out of the way until you need them.
Chart labels now show dates, not week numbers
The capacity vs demand trend chart X-axis now displays short dates (e.g. "27 Jul") instead of "Wk 31". No more mentally mapping week numbers to actual dates.
Removed clutter
The short-days pill row, stat chips (supply %, demand met, capacity lost), and the status/greeting narrative block have all been removed. The page is now significantly less noisy.
Page 2
Capacity Outlook
Stage badges always visible
PVG, REF1, and REF2 stage badges are now always shown on every joiner row — red when not yet completed, green once saved. You can instantly see what's outstanding without expanding a row.
KPI cards reordered
The four summary cards are now ordered to match the natural workflow: In Pipeline → Available Changes → Staff Leaving → Running Net. The grid is also tightened from 5 to 4 columns.
Risk card removed
The standalone Risk card has been removed from the KPI row to reduce noise. Risk context is still available within individual row data.
Confidence % removed from joiner modal
The Confidence % field has been removed from the joiner edit modal. It was creating unnecessary friction and has been simplified out of the workflow.

These changes are live on your dashboard now — just refresh to see them.

## Big picture first (so the logic makes sense)

Your automation replaces this entire manual flow:

1. Go to People Planner
2. Run three different exports
3. Download three Excel files
4. Upload them into your dashboard

Instead, the automation will:

* Download those same three exports
* Save them with **exact filenames**
* Put them in a known location
* Let your dashboard process them automatically

So the **contract** between automation and dashboard is purely file-based.

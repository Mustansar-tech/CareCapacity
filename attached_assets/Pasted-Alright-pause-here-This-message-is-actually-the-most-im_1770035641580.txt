## What an “empty screenshot” really means

When Playwright saves a PNG and it’s **blank / white / empty**, it means one of these is true:

1. The page is still `about:blank`
2. The browser was redirected to a page that **refuses to render in headless**
3. The page is blocked by security headers (X-Frame / CSP / bot protection)
4. Chromium loaded something, but **nothing was painted**

Given everything we’ve seen, it is **#2 and #3 combined**.

## Why all selectors are timing out

Subject: Care Capacity Dashboard — Redesigned Home & Capacity Outlook

Hi team,

We've redesigned the Home dashboard and Capacity Outlook pages. If you've logged in recently you'll already see the new look — here's a breakdown of exactly what changed and why.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HOME DASHBOARD — BEFORE & AFTER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

BEFORE
  • Large centred "Welcome to Care Capacity Dashboard" banner taking up
    the top quarter of the screen
  • Upload and Process buttons on their own separate row
  • "Select Week:" label with a standalone week picker below that
  • Two rows of 10 KPI tiles (Desired Hours, Unavailability, Sickness,
    Holidays, Net Capacity, Domiciliary Hours, Client Scheduled,
    Other Scheduled, Capacity After Scheduling, GH Loss)

AFTER
  ✓ New compact hero header:
    — "This week at a glance" pill badge
    — "Welcome back — [Branch Name]" headline with your branch name
      highlighted in green — instantly tells you which branch you're on
    — One-line subtitle beneath it

  ✓ Live capacity vs demand trend chart sits alongside the header,
    showing all processed weeks plus a 1-week forecast at a glance.
    Chart labels now show real dates (e.g. "27 Jul") instead of
    week numbers like "Wk 31".

  ✓ Controls are now one clean row pinned to the bottom of the header:
    Upload New Data · Process Data · week navigation · last-sync time.
    No more hunting across the page for these.

  ✓ Removed noise: the short-days pill row, stat chips (supply %,
    demand met, capacity lost), and the old status/greeting narrative
    have all been stripped out.

  ✓ KPI tiles remain below the header, unchanged in data — just
    easier to reach now that the header doesn't dominate.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CAPACITY OUTLOOK — BEFORE & AFTER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

BEFORE
  • 5-column KPI card grid (In Pipeline, Staff Leaving, Risk, Running Net,
    Available Changes — in no particular workflow order)
  • A standalone Risk card shown in the KPI row
  • Stage badges (PVG, REF1, REF2) only visible when a stage was active —
    you couldn't tell at a glance what was still outstanding
  • Joiner edit modal included a Confidence % field

AFTER
  ✓ KPI cards reordered to match your actual workflow:
    In Pipeline → Available Changes → Staff Leaving → Running Net
    Grid tightened from 5 to 4 columns.

  ✓ Risk card removed from the KPI row — reduces visual noise.

  ✓ Stage badges always visible on every joiner row:
    — RED  = PVG / REF1 / REF2 not yet completed
    — GREEN = stage saved and complete
    No more expanding rows to check what's outstanding.

  ✓ Confidence % field removed from the joiner edit modal.
    Simplified the data entry to only what's needed.

Everything is live now — just refresh your browser.
No changes to your data, only the layout and presentation.
